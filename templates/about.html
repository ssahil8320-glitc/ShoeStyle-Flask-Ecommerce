OUR STORY

Made for your everyday.

ShoeStyle is a demo footwear store concept built around self-expression, comfort and the joy of finding the right pair.

ONE STEP AT A TIME

Style should move with you.

From everyday essentials to playful kids' shoes and sporty favourites, ShoeStyle brings different footwear styles together in one simple shopping experience.

This project is a Flask + SQLite learning storefront. Product images and sample inventory are illustrative; real fulfilment and payment services need to be configured before launch.
